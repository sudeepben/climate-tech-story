Data Analysis + Communication

At EVE HealthCare, I learned that analysis only matters when people can use it.

Operational scheduling, capacity data, bottlenecks, and KPI reporting

  • The team needed a clearer view of scheduling and capacity patterns.
  • I analyzed exception patterns and recurring bottlenecks.
  • I built Power BI KPI reporting with standardized definitions and drilldowns.
  • The goal was to help leadership see what needed attention first.
Presenter cue Tell this as moving from numbers to a useful conversation.
Impact

The impact was a clearer decision conversation.

  • Leadership could monitor trends and investigate outliers faster.
  • Standardized KPI definitions reduced ambiguity.
  • Planning discussions became easier to compare and prioritize.
  • The work turned operational data into recommendations, not just reports.
